## Case 26, video (the main one): FAIL

Sending from dev: plus → «Фото или видео» → the test video picked (colour bars,
0:04) → confirm. The message went out with ✓✓ and a sharp preview in the feed.

Playback does not work on either device:

- dev (the sender): tapping the preview opens the fullscreen player with a black
  screen and a crossed-out play icon. Three screenshots taken 1.5 s apart are
  byte-identical (md5 6ed18674… on all three), so the frame never changes. A tap
  on the screen brought up the AVPlayer controls with the timeline reading
  `--:-- … --:--`, meaning the AVPlayerItem never loaded at all. Tapping Play
  closed the player: a white transition frame, then the chat list.
- Pro Max (the receiver): the video arrived and the preview is sharp. Tapping the
  preview gives the same black screen with the crossed-out play, and three frames
  1.5 s apart are again identical (md5 17cccc91… on all three).

So delivery and previews work while decoding and opening the file in the player
do not. The symptom matches the class of bug from commit 4747a9a («AVPlayer
can't open extensionless video/audio»), but that is a match by symptom only; I
did not check the code.

An observation alongside: the chat ended up with two identical videos at 12:56
and 12:58 from a single send confirmation. Other agents were working on the dev
simulator during the first half of the run (see interference below), so I cannot
attribute the duplicate with any confidence.

## Critical bug found along the way: after an app restart the chat opens empty and input is dead

Reproduction on dev:

1. At around 13:05 the app on dev died (the process disappeared; this coincided
   in time with another process installing MsngrUITests-Runner onto the
   simulator, see interference below, causation not proven).
2. After `simctl launch` the chat list is correct and previews and badges are
   alive, but chat 445566 opens completely empty, not a single message. The
   Design Reviewer request chat is empty too, while its badge reads «2».
3. The input field does not take focus: tapping it does not raise the keyboard
   and `idb ui text` types nothing. The plus menu and attachment sending do work
   (the photo went out), and marking as read on opening a chat works as well.
4. Neither terminate plus launch nor reinstalling the build on top cures it.
   Swipes do not help. There are no errors in the app log, and `/api/chats`
   answers 200.

So the local data is there, the list can see it, while the feed and the input in
a chat are dead. It blocks any work with history on the device after a restart.

### Case 42, spam: PASS

Ten messages sent from dev back to back at 18:38, in a loop of tap the field,
`idb ui text`, tap send. All 10 show ✓✓ at the sender and all 10 arrived on Pro
Max in the correct order, 1 through 10. The UI did not hang: the feed on Pro Max
scrolls into history and back, autoscroll to new messages works, and the
scroll-to-bottom button appears at the sender after sending.

Two artefacts of my input, not app bugs: keyboard autocapitalisation («Spam-N»
instead of «spam-N»), and message 2 having its text cut down to «Sp» because
`idb text` raced the send tap. Neither affects order or delivery: message 2 is
identified by its position between Spam-1 and Spam-3 and arrived in place.

### Case 13, simultaneous reactions: PASS

Target message Spam-10, visible on both. A 0.8 s long press on dev, then ❤️ from
the reaction row; immediately after, a long press on Pro Max on the same message,
then 👍. By the time the menu opened on Pro Max, the ❤️ from dev was already
showing in the preview. Three seconds later both reactions (❤️ and 👍) are
visible under Spam-10 on both devices. No conflict and no overwriting.

At the end both simulators were left running, with both apps back on the chat
list.
