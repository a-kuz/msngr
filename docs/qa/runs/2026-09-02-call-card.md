# The live call card: placed, kept current, joined from, closed

2026-09-02, three simulators on the shared stand: `charlie` (fixture) on
fable-a as the host, two throwaway accounts registered with `msngrfixture`
on fable-b (the primary peer) and fable-c (the invited one), each with a
direct chat to charlie.

## What was checked

1. charlie dials fable-b from their chat header; fable-b answers. On the call
   screen charlie taps the add button and picks fable-c.
2. fable-c rings and declines. Its chat with charlie holds the system line
   «Charlie Service пригласил(а) вас в звонок» and, under it, the card
   «Групповой звонок» with the two people still in the call
   (`2026-09-02-call-card-declined.png`). charlie's database holds one card
   per chat, both edited down to two members after the decline:

   ```
   seq 9  (chat with fable-c)  members: Charlie Service, Delta Service
   seq 14 (chat with fable-b)  members: Charlie Service, Delta Service
   ```

3. fable-c taps the card. Its call screen comes up as a three-party call
   («Charlie Service, Delta Service», the clock running); charlie's screen
   shows two peers (`…-joined.png`, `…-joined-host.png`). Both of charlie's
   cards now list three members, through edits of the same rows.
4. charlie hangs up. Six seconds later every copy of the card carries
   `endedAt` — charlie's two, fable-b's one, fable-c's one — and the row
   reads «Групповой звонок завершён» with the people and the length
   (`…-ended.png`); the chat list previews it the same way.

## Found and fixed along the way

- An incoming card was stored as a text row and showed its raw JSON: the
  receive path mapped only `callLog` to the `.call` row kind. Both the live
  and the history paths now take `callLive` the same way (unit
  `testIncomingCardLandsAsACallRow`). The first, unfixed card of the run is
  the JSON bubble visible above the real one in `…-ended.png`.
- A card whose writer's app died mid-call stayed live forever. A participant
  whose own call ends now closes its copies locally
  (`closeCallLiveLocally`; unit `testACallEndingClosesItsCardsLocally`).
- The card carried the «изм.» mark after every edit, and the bubble's time
  ran over the detail line: a `.call` row shows no edited mark, the card
  takes the wide slot, and the clock leads the detail line so a long list of
  names is what gets cut, never the time.

## Units

`CallManagerTests`: the inviter leaves the card in both chats; hanging up
closes every card once; joining from a card dials the writer and every other
member and refuses an ended card; a participant's own call ending closes its
copies. `CallSignalTests`: the card is service with a row, round-trips, and
never reads as a call log.
