# Run: two-finger pinch zoom in the media viewer, 2026-09-01

The double tap 1 ↔ 2.5 was already live (qa/runs/2026-08-27-viewer); the pinch
itself had been waiting for a two-finger driver. Simulator.app synthesizes one:
holding ⌥ turns the pointer into two mirrored touches, and cliclick drives the
drag, so the gesture is a real two-finger pinch delivered through UIKit.

Device: an own simulator (iPhone 17, iOS 26.5), the alfa fixture, the photo
message in the «Rc» chat.

1. The photo opens fitted — `2026-09-01-pinch/before.png`.
2. ⌥-drag outward from the picture's center: the image follows the fingers
   into a deep zoom, far past the double-tap's 2.5 —
   `2026-09-01-pinch/after.png`.
3. ⌥-drag back inward: the scale comes back down and snaps to the fit at the
   1.05 threshold; the tail of the same drag, now at scale 1, reads as the
   swipe-down and closes the viewer — the ordinary dismiss, not a defect.

Verdict: pinch zoom is live in both directions; the roadmap line is done.
